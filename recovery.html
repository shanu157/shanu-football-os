RECOVERY CENTER

Rest is part of becoming a better footballer. Listen to your body.

🌙

Sleep

Keep a regular sleep routine. Teenagers generally need 8–10 hours.

💧

Hydration

Drink water regularly, including around football sessions.

🥗

Nutrition

Eat balanced meals with enough energy to support training and growth.

🧘

Mobility

Choose comfortable, gentle mobility work. Avoid movements that cause pain.
